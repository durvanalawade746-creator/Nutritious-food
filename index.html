Nutrition Science

Foundations of Healthy Eating

Learn about the essential building blocks of a balanced, wholesome daily diet.

🍛

Balanced Diet

A balanced diet includes all essential macronutrients and micronutrients in proper proportions—ensuring your body receives carbohydrates, proteins, healthy fats, vitamins, and minerals without excess calories.

💪

Protein

Crucial for muscle repair, cell growth, and immune strength. Plant-based Indian sources include dal, rajma, chole, paneer, sprouts, moong dal chilla, and nuts.

🌾

Complex Carbohydrates

Carbs are your primary energy source. Choose complex carbs like brown rice, oats, millet, ragi, and whole wheat rotis over refined white flour to maintain steady glucose levels.

🥑

Healthy Fats

Essential for brain health, hormone synthesis, and vitamin absorption. Incorporate modest amounts of pure ghee, nuts, seeds, mustard oil, and groundnuts.

🍊

Vitamins & Minerals

Micronutrients safeguard against illness and support metabolic functions. Citrus fruits, leafy green spinach, carrots, tomatoes, and amla are powerhouses of immunity.

🥦

Dietary Fiber

Fiber promotes digestive regularity, regulates blood sugar, and keeps you feeling full longer. Whole grains, legumes, raw vegetables, and fruits are packed with fiber.

💧

Hydration

Water transports nutrients, lubricates joints, and regulates body temperature. Drink at least 2 to 2.5 liters of water daily, complemented by buttermilk and coconut water.

🍎

Fruits & Vegetables

Aim for 5 colorful servings daily. Eating seasonal produce supplies antioxidants that fight oxidative stress and keep your energy levels soaring.

⏰

Regular Meal Timing

Skipping meals leads to fatigue and overeating later. Eating breakfast, lunch, snack, and dinner at consistent times supports a healthy metabolic rhythm.

Smart Choices

Nourishing vs Less-Nourishing Foods

Simple swaps you can make every day for lasting energy and wellness.

✅ Nourishing Choices
🌱 Whole Grains: Poha, Upma, Brown Rice, Ragi Dosa, Multigrain Roti
🥗 Smart Snacks: Roasted Makhana, Sprouts Chaat, Roasted Chickpeas, Fresh Fruit
🍵 Healthy Drinks: Tender Coconut Water, Fresh Masala Buttermilk, Water
🍲 Home-cooked Proteins: Dal Tadka, Rajma Curry, Paneer Bhurji, Moong Dal Khichdi
⚠️ Less-Nourishing Choices
🍞 Refined Carbs: White Bread, Maida Pastries, Highly Processed Biscuits
🍟 Ultra-Processed Snacks: Deep-fried Packaged Chips, Instant Noodles with excess sodium
🥤 Sugary Beverages: Carbonated Soft Drinks, Concentrated Packaged Juices
🍔 Heavy Fried Foods: Frequent Fast-food Meals rich in trans fats and artificial preservatives
